# Orchestrator concept notes (noonautics pass) — written 2026-10-21 ~02:40 while research agents run

## 1. Locate the stuckness
Henrique has four artifacts that feel like one thing but have no shared name or shape:
(a) curriculum = operating the instrument (AI 101→601); (b) Stack = the architecture of a delegation;
(c) Specifying guide = the writing method; (d) Archetypes = the stance the human takes.
Calculator analogy: (a) is "how to press the buttons"; (d) is "knowing what you are actually asking the calculator to do and when
it is the wrong tool". The missing meta is whatever makes (a)+(b)+(c)+(d) one discipline.

## 2. Stratonautics (climb by asking why)
Why operate well? → to get the right result with less effort. Why know your stance? → to not be betrayed by the tool's
failure mode. Why the Stack? → to diagnose at the right layer. Common genus: ALL FOUR ARE THE HUMAN SIDE OF A DELEGATION
TO A NON-HUMAN INTELLIGENCE. Climb once more: who decides, who holds the purpose, who is accountable.
Evidence from the corpus itself:
- Stack doc: the regress "is terminated by human governance... a human who says: this is what we value".
- Archetypes: "delegation transfers labor, never responsibility".
- Sivulka: "Someone still has to tell them both what to do."
- Nadella: "what you create should belong to you".
- Rieseberg: the limit is the human's ambition/self-efficacy.
=> The meta-discipline: BEING THE ACCOUNTABLE HUMAN WHO DIRECTS MACHINE INTELLIGENCE.
   In school terms: you may use the calculator, but YOU sign the exam.

## 3. Transnautics (who already solved "direct a capable, literal, partly unreliable agent and stay accountable")
1. PRINCIPAL–AGENT THEORY (economics). The human is literally the PRINCIPAL; the software is literally the AGENT. The field's
   three problems map onto the Stack: information asymmetry → context; objective divergence → intent; costly monitoring →
   judgment/evaluation; moral hazard → Executor complacency (inverted: it is the principal who slacks). Adverse selection →
   choosing the archetype/model. Negative transfer: LLM agents are not self-interested; divergence comes from default priors,
   not incentives. (Sivulka anthropomorphizes it anyway: "tokens build empires".)
2. MISSION COMMAND / COMMANDER'S INTENT (military doctrine, Auftragstaktik). Give the WHY and the END STATE and the
   CONSTRAINTS, not the HOW. This is intent engineering's historical home and the Specifying guide's spirit. Use as teaching
   import, not as brand (too martial for office workers).
3. AVIATION AUTOMATION. Pilot-in-command rule (responsibility never transfers to the autopilot), automation complacency,
   mode confusion ("mode fluency" is Henrique's own phrase), ratings ladder (student → private → instrument → commercial → ATP)
   ≈ AI 101→601. "Instrument-rated" = certified to fly when you cannot see outside = the Executor archetype (work happens
   where you are not looking). R4 is gathering the evidence.
4. CALCULATORS IN MATH EDUCATION. The curriculum moved from arithmetic to modeling; "when to reach for it" became the skill.
5. FILM DIRECTION / ORCHESTRA CONDUCTING. The director/conductor does not operate the instrument; works through intent and
   interpretation; is responsible for the whole. "Orchestrator" is already the software term; the human above it is the conductor.
   Also: railway conductor (Sivulka's railroad story: "make the trains run on time").
6. HORSEMANSHIP. The original intelligent agent you direct with reins; it has its own judgment and can bolt. Niche.
7. LAW. ABA Formal Opinion 512 (2024): lawyers must supervise generative AI as they supervise non-lawyer assistants. A real
   "accountability stays with the human" rule. (R4/R2 may verify.)

Henrique's binding constraint is not prompt or context (abundant content). It is INTENT (what does the venture want: income,
reputation, transition?) and COHERENCE (five artifacts, three names: agentic-engineering, AIES, PonicLabs). The strategy brief's
first job is intent + coherence engineering for the brand. Reflexive hook for the site: "we ran our own audit protocol on ourselves".

NEW ARTIFACT FOUND: the Archetypes and the Stack describe the same delegation from two sides. Archetype = the contract the
human THINKS they have. Stack layer = what the human must actually SUPPLY for that contract to hold. Cross-table (stance × layer):
  Oracle → Judgment (doubt) | Genie → Prompt (literalism) | Interpreter → Evaluation (evidence trail) | Executor → Harness+Judgment

## 5. Chrononautics
Genealogy of the words: prompt eng (2022) → context eng (mid-2025, Karpathy/Lütke) → intent eng (Nate Jones) → harness eng
(2026) → agentic eng (Karpathy, Feb 2026). Each new word = one layer. Ghost path: "computer literacy" → "computational thinking"
(Papert 1980, Wing 2006): literacy ≠ thinking. Constraint change that reopens the path: EU AI Act Art. 4 makes "AI literacy"
a legal duty of every deployer (R2 verifying) → SMEs must buy exactly this, and most offerings are button-pressing courses.
Adjacent possible now open: skills/plugins marketplaces make a skill pack distributable; agents make the Executor stance the
default by 2027, so the scarce skill is the one Henrique already teaches (Microsoft calls it "agent boss").

## 6. Scoponautics (right grain)
Too fine: the prompt (commoditized courses). Too coarse: enterprise transformation (McKinsey's grain; unwinnable solo).
Right grain for SMEs: ONE RECURRING WORKFLOW WITH AN OWNER, made reliably delegable (exactly what AI 401's folder structure is).
Product unit = "one delegated workflow, harnessed and evaluated, plus the team taught the stance". Sivulka: "record the loops that work".

## 7. Follow the surprise → candidate meta-names
Biggest surprise: PRINCIPAL. Exact dual of "agent"; carries accountability (law), ownership (finance: the principal is what you
must not lose — Nadella), school (the calculator analogy), seniority (principal engineer). Counter-position to "user" and to
"copilot": you are not the user, you are the principal. Risks: Principal Financial Group (insurer) trademark in other classes;
"principal" is an ordinary word.
Second: CONDUCTOR (above the orchestrator; railway double meaning). Third: PILOT IN COMMAND (strong doctrine, but "pilot"
is saturated by Copilot). Fourth: keep AGENTIC ENGINEERING for the technical slice; it now rides Karpathy's generic term (SEO
upside, no ownership).

## 8. The unified model (draft) — "four axes of one space"
The four materials are four axes of one space, and the meta-skill is deliberate movement in it:
  STANCE (which archetype am I in, and what is its failure mode?)              ← Archetypes
  STRUCTURE (which Stack layer carries the load; which is my binding constraint?) ← AIES + Specifying
  AUTONOMY (assistive → directed → autonomous → continuous; how much do I watch?) ← Specifying guide modes + curriculum ladder
  AMBITION (speed / altitude / depth / range; how big is the ask?)             ← Rieseberg
"Mode fluency" (Henrique's phrase) generalizes from "which layer failed" to "where am I in this space and should I be here?"
The curriculum walks the AUTONOMY axis; the business sells movement on all four.
