§1 The four axes

One space, four axes

The Stances, the Stack, the Specifying guide with the AI @ Workplace curriculum, and Felix Rieseberg’s ambition types are not separate things. Together they map four axes of one space. Mode fluency is the skill of moving through it on purpose, and re-entering at the right point when the work goes wrong.

Axis 01 · The Fourteen Stances

What am I expecting from this exchange?

Oracle, Genie, Tutor, Mirror, Adversary, Librarian, Interpreter, Simulator, Scribe, Executor, Muse, Miner, Therapist, Translator.

Axis 02 · The AI-Human Engineering Stack

What must I supply for that expectation to hold?

Prompt, Context, Intent, Judgment, Coherence; plus two meta-functions, Evaluation and Harness.

Axis 03 · Modes, from Specifying Work for AI

How much of the work do I watch?

Assistive (you type, AI suggests), Directed (you describe, AI types, you review), Autonomous (you assign, AI executes, you review), Continuous (AI runs as a background service).

§2 The Fourteen Stances

What you think you asked for

A stance is the contract you believe you have with the machine. Each one fails in its own way, and the failure is easy to miss from inside it. Here they are, one at a time.

  1. 01OracleTruth to a hard questionFails as confident plausible prose taken as fact
  2. 02GenieA discrete deliverableFails as literal compliance with a brittle wish
  3. 03TutorA path to the answerFails as being taught toward a goal you never stated
  4. 04MirrorMy own thinking, clarifiedFails as flattery mistaken for reflection
  5. 05AdversaryAttack on my planFails as critique of the wrong thing
  6. 06LibrarianSynthesis of what is knownFails as confident retrieval of what was never said
  7. 07InterpreterDecode material I supplyFails as confabulated meaning that sounds insightful
  8. 08SimulatorA rehearsal of realityFails as a rehearsal of a scenario that is not yours
  9. 09ScribeCraft applied to my substanceFails as polished form over a hollow draft
  10. 10ExecutorA delegated job, reviewed afterFails as automation complacency; locally correct steps compounding into the wrong outcome, found late
  11. 11MuseProvocation, not correctnessFails as nothing: there is no failure, only dross
  12. 12MinerThe shape of a space, not one answerFails as believing an anomaly because you wanted it; losing the vein
  13. 13TherapistA space to think aloud (or Confessor)Fails as dependency; outsourcing judgment about your own life
  14. 14TranslatorA bridge between two worldsFails as a bridge from one world to a caricature of the other

§3 The matrix

Each stance leans on one layer

A stance is the contract you think you have with the AI. A Stack layer is what you must actually supply for that contract to hold. Each stance leans on one layer more than the others; that is where its failure lives, and where the guardrail belongs.

§4 Mode shift

One request, three contracts

The words you type are the last step. Name the stance first and the sentence changes, because the thing you must supply changes. The plain request: Why are our customers cancelling?

Before you answer: how certain can you be about why our customers cancel, and what is that certainty based on?
