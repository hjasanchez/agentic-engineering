is the practice of working with AI deliberately: knowing which stance you are in, what you must supply for it to hold, how much of the work you are watching, and how big an ask you are making. §1 four_axes

One space, four axes

The Stances, the Stack, the Specifying guide with the AI @ Workplace curriculum, and Felix Rieseberg's ambition types are not separate things. Together they map four axes of one space. is the skill of moving through it on purpose, and re-entering at the right point when the work goes wrong.

  1. AXIS 01 N · The Fourteen Stances

    Stance

    What am I expecting from this exchange?

    Oracle, Genie, Tutor, Mirror, Adversary, Librarian, Interpreter, Simulator, Scribe, Executor, Muse, Miner, Therapist, Translator.

    See all fourteen stances →

  2. AXIS 02 E · The AI-Human Engineering Stack

    Structure

    What must I supply for that expectation to hold?

    Prompt, Context, Intent, Judgment, Coherence; plus two meta-functions, Evaluation and Harness.

    Walk the stack →

  3. AXIS 03 S · Modes, from Specifying Work for AI

    Autonomy

    How much of the work do I watch?

    Assistive (you type, AI suggests), Directed (you describe, AI types, you review), Autonomous (you assign, AI executes, you review), Continuous (AI runs as a background service).

    Autonomy and spec rigor →

    Ambition

    Speed, Altitude, Depth, Range.
