Research date: 2026-10-29. Brief: `strategy/briefs/W25_scalable_products.md`. Inputs: `OFFER_BRAINSTORM.md`, `R2_market_and_monetization.md`,
`R5_footprint_audit.md`, `R6_one_off_offers.md`, the repo's published assets. R10 (harness kits) does not exist yet, so kit content is
treated as planned, not built.

**How to read this.**
- "Accessed" is the date I read the live page. *Snippet only* means the figure came from a search-result summary and I could not
  confirm it on the page: those are **UNVERIFIED**. Rows carried over from R2 or R6 keep their original access dates.
- Most benchmark pages are vendors or affiliates. Each is one data point, not a market average.
- Build hours, ongoing hours and volumes are my planning estimates, not measurements. Reach is the R5 baseline (2026-10-21): 71 GitHub
  stars, 441 views across the four AI @ Workplace videos, LinkedIn as the only active channel (follower count unknown).
- **Volume assumption used throughout:** over 12 months, 300 (low) to 2,000 (high) people see a given paid offer, through the site,
  LinkedIn, the repo and YouTube. Self-serve conversion of 1–2% (R2: ~2% tech newsletter free-to-paid; course-creator rule of thumb 1–2%,
  https://paigebrunton.com/blog/email-list-size-online-course , accessed 2026-10-29, not an independent benchmark).
- All bands are internal. Nothing here goes on the site or in the README (decision of 2026-10-29).
- "No marginal cost" is never zero below: every row lists the platform fee and the hours a month it still takes.

chapter, four short self-checks and the Specifying templates. Sold on Gumroad or Teachable.

**By strategic value (feeds the ladder 0–3 + builds the audience 0–3):**
1. **L Offline team kit (5)**: every sale puts 5–10 people through the free Diagnostic and ends at the Team read and the assessment.
2. **G Newsletter (5)**: the free list is the audience Henrique lacks (R5); the paid tier is incidental.
3. **E Article 4 kit (4)**: each buyer is a qualified lead for the Article 4 pack and the assessment, and the template is shared with both.
   (K and H also score 4; E wins the tie on hours and has no decision conflict.)
Then A, C, F (3); D, J (2); M (1); I (0).

**Single best first build: E, the Article 4 do-it-yourself records kit.** It leads the ranking once ongoing hours count, ranks third on
strategy, conflicts with no decision on file, and its core template is the one the Article 4 pack and the assessment already need, so
the 15–20 hours are not spent on the kit alone. Credit its price against the Article 4 pack. It is a small product: the realistic year
is $100–2,500, and its job is to turn a search for "Article 4" into a conversation.
