Why not just a workshop?

Because training alone does not fix the hardest failures. Automation bias “cannot be prevented by training or instructions”, and complacency affects experts as well as novices (Parasuraman & Manzey, Human Factors, 2010). The assessment installs structure: checkpoints, evaluation, a harness. The workshop teaches people to use it.

About Article 4, honestly

Since July 2026 (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744), Article 4 of the EU AI Act says providers and deployers “shall take measures to support the development of AI literacy of their staff”, and that this “does not require providers or deployers to guarantee any specific level of AI literacy of any individual.” National enforcement has applied since 2 August 2026. According to the European Commission's AI Literacy Q&A, no certificate is required and internal records of training and other initiatives are enough evidence.

So I will not sell you a certificate, and I will not sell you a fear of fines. The evidence pack is a clear record of proportionate measures, tailored to your tools and roles. This is not legal advice.

Sources: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, OJ L, 24 July 2026; European Commission, AI Literacy Questions & Answers (updated 20 October 2026).
