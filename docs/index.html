A practice by Henrique Jr. Sanchez

Know which mode you're in.

We taught everyone to press the buttons. Nobody taught them which problems the calculator is for.

Mode fluency is the practice of working with AI deliberately: knowing which stance you are in, what you must supply for it to hold, how much of the work you are watching, and how big an ask you are making. It is for people who already use AI every day and still get burned, and for the teams that have to show they did something about it.

§1 The four axes

One space, four axes

The Stances, the Stack, the Specifying guide with the AI @ Workplace curriculum, and Felix Rieseberg's ambition types are not separate things. Together they map four axes of one space. Mode fluency is the skill of moving through it on purpose, and re-entering at the right point when the work goes wrong.

§3 The matrix

Each stance leans on one layer

A stance is the contract you think you have with the AI. A Stack layer is what you must actually supply for that contract to hold. Each stance leans on one layer more than the others; that is where its failure lives, and where the guardrail belongs. Four of fourteen rows:

Excerpt from the Mode Fluency Matrix
StanceWhat you expectLoad-bearing layerGuardrail
OracleTruth to a hard questionJudgmentAsk for the uncertainty before the answer. Treat the answer as a claim, not a verdict.
GenieA discrete deliverablePromptSpecify scope, boundaries and success criteria before you ask (an Intent Doc).
InterpreterDecode material I supplyEvaluation“Show me exactly which parts of the text support each conclusion.” Never accept an interpretation you cannot trace.
ExecutorA delegated job, reviewed afterHarness + JudgmentPlan before action, checkpoints, a deviation report, nothing consequential ships uninspected. Delegation transfers labor, never responsibility.
