    The interactive console needs JavaScript. Here are the same questions, so you can run the diagnostic on paper: the first questions lead to your stance; then look it up in the matrix and read across to its layer and guardrail.

    the questions

    1. In this task, what do you mostly want from the AI?
      • An answer, or a fact
      • A thing made, or a job done
      • Help understanding something
      • Help thinking, practising or deciding
    2. If you chose “An answer, or a fact”: Where should the answer come from?
      • From the AI itself: I want the truth about a hard question I can't easily check myself → Oracle
      • From what is already known or published: find it and pull it together → Librarian
    3. If you chose “A thing made, or a job done”: What do you hand over?
      • My own rough material (notes, bullets, a draft), to be polished or formatted → Scribe
      • A request for one deliverable, which I check when it arrives → Genie
      • A goal: the AI works out the steps and does them, and I review at the end → Executor
    4. If you chose “Help understanding something”: Which is closer?
      • I have the material (a contract, a report, a dataset): tell me what it means and what follows from it → Interpreter
      • I want to understand it myself, and keep that understanding → Tutor
      • I need to carry it between two worlds (the engineer to the marketer, the doctor to the patient) → Translator
    5. If you chose “Help thinking, practising or deciding”: Which is closer?
      • Attack my plan or draft: find the holes → Adversary
      • Rehearse something real: a negotiation, a difficult conversation, a market → Simulator
      • Reflect my own thinking back to me, with more structure → Mirror
      • A non-judgemental space to think aloud about something personal → Therapist
      • Ideas: surprise me
    6. If you chose “Ideas: surprise me”: How do you want the ideas?
      • One good spark, which I judge as soon as it arrives → Muse
      • Many variations, kept, and sorted later with a cold eye → Miner
    7. How long have you used AI tools for work?
      • I'm new to them, or have barely tried
      • Weeks to months of chatbot use
      • Daily chatbot use, maybe custom bots or projects
      • I use agents that take actions (files, tools, other apps)
    8. In this task, how much of the work do you watch?
      • Assistive: I type, the AI suggests
      • Directed: I describe, the AI types, I review
      • Autonomous: I assign, the AI executes, I review the result
      • Continuous: the AI runs as a background service
    9. How big is the ask?
      • Speed: the same work in less time
      • Altitude: the outcome beyond the task
      • Depth: the task done the way a field-leading expert would
      • Range: bring in other fields, tools and techniques
    10. Last time the output disappointed you, what did you do first?
      • Rewrote or reworded the prompt
      • Added files, examples or background information
      • Explained what mattered most, or which tradeoff to make
      • Checked it against a source, a test or the original
      • Gave up and did it myself
    11. Before the output leaves under your name, how do you usually check it?
      • I don't, usually
      • I ask the same chat to check its work
      • I paste it into a fresh chat and ask it to find errors
      • I run it past a different model
      • I check it against the source, or my own calculation
      • I ask a person with the expertise I lack
